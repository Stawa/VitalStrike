# Code of Conduct

Welcome to the community of VitalStrike. We aim to create a friendly and secure community where everyone may work together on this project. We appreciate collaboration and respect for everyone else.

## Standards

All members of the VitalStrike community are expected to comply with the following standards:

- **Be Respectful**: Be kind and respectful to everyone.
- **Be Collaborative**: Collaborate positively to make the project better.
- **Be Patient**: Acknowledge that the experience levels of contributors differ.
- **Be Constructive**: Offer honest feedback that concentrates on the concept or code rather than the individual.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following actions are not accepted in order to help maintain a positive environment:

- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling, spamming, or disruptive behavior.
- Personal attacks or publishing others' private information without permission.
- Incitement of violence or promotion of illegal activities.

## Reporting Issues

If you see or encounter inappropriate activity, please report it right away. We are dedicated to immediately and fairly resolving all issues that are reported.
